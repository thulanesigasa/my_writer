## Step 1: The Hook & Context
- **Objective:** Grab the reader's attention immediately without starting with definitions.
- **Execution:** Open with a micro-scenario, a dramatic historical event, or an intriguing narrative paradox.
- **Rule:** Do NOT use dictionary definitions ("Dictionary.com defines instinct as..."). Drop the reader straight into the narrative tension.

## Step 2: The Hard Science
- **Objective:** Establish unassailable scientific rigour.
- **Execution:** Detail the exact brain structures (e.g., vmPFC, anterior insula, DMN), hormones/neurotransmitters (e.g., oxytocin, cortisol, BDNF), and physiological pathways involved.
- **Rule:** Use approved biological terms from `system_rules.md`. Never invent or hallucinate studies or statistics.

## Step 3: The Analogy
- **Objective:** Anchor the complex biological mechanism in sensory, intuitive reality.
- **Execution:** Provide a vivid, original metaphor (e.g., sheet music annotations for epigenetics, city traffic for the Default Mode Network).
- **Rule:** Comply with the Analogy Quality Test in `stylistic_examples.md`. Never use banned clichés (iceberg, orchestra, GPS).

## Step 4: The Case Study
- **Objective:** Provide empirical proof of the concept in action.
- **Execution:** Draw directly from the authoritative narrative source material in `case_studies.md` (e.g., Phineas Gage, EVR, Iowa Gambling Task, Gary Klein's firefighters, Jazz improvisation fMRI).
- **Rule:** Retain absolute factual accuracy regarding names, dates, and experimental setups.

## Step 5: The Counter-Argument / Misconception
- **Objective:** Protect the reader from bad science and pop-psychology tropes.
- **Execution:** Identify what pop culture or traditional management got wrong (e.g., debunking the Triune/Lizard Brain myth, showing why oxytocin is not a pure "love hormone").
- **Rule:** Explicitly state the myth, then immediately provide the corrective scientific reality.

## Step 6: The Practical Application
- **Objective:** Translate the scientific insight into immediate value for the target reader.
- **Execution:** Address Jordan (The Stressed Leader) and Sam (The Personal Growth Seeker) directly. Detail how to use this knowledge in decision-making, team design, or personal habit formation.
- **Rule:** Offer concrete, actionable protocols rather than vague encouragement.

# Sub-Section Word Budget & Pacing

To maintain an expansive 2,500-word target per chapter across 3 sub-sections:

- Each full 6-step cycle should span **700 to 850 words**.
- Step 1 (Hook & Context): ~100–150 words
- Step 2 (Hard Science): ~150–200 words
- Step 3 (The Analogy): ~100–150 words
- Step 4 (The Case Study): ~150–200 words
- Step 5 (Counter-Argument): ~100–150 words
- Step 6 (Practical Application): ~100–150 words

By following this breakdown, every concept receives thorough, comprehensive treatment with zero filler.
